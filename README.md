## Business Problem

Wanjiku operates a fresh produce stall at Gikomba Market and wants to understand how her business is performing. Using two weeks of sales and stock data, this analysis evaluates sales performance, business growth, inventory levels, and product profitability. The findings are used to provide actionable recommendations that can help improve revenue and support better inventory decisions.

## Top Three Findings

1. Total sales over 14 days were KES 46,450.
2. Week 2 sales grew by 10.66% compared to Week 1.
3. Average weekend sales (KES 4,900) were 69.84% higher than average weekday sales (KES 2,885).
